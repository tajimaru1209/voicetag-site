VoiceTag Studio

Photo stickers that talk.

Made on your iPhone · No account · No ads

How it works

1. Choose a photo

Tap +, then Choose a Photo. The subject is cut out automatically as a sticker.

2. Hold to record

Press and hold the microphone, and let go when you're done. You can record up to 30 seconds. Tap play to listen, or hold again for a new take.

3. Save, then tap to hear it

Add a title and tap Save VoiceTag. Tap any sticker on your page to hear it again. Touch and hold a sticker to delete it.

4. Share it as a video

Tap the share button on a sticker's card. VoiceTag Studio makes a short video, and you can send it in a message or choose Save Video.

Questions

The cutout missed part of my photo

Cutouts work best when the person, pet, or object is close up and stands out from the background. Tap Change Photo to try another.

Nothing records when I hold the button

The first time, iOS asks to use the microphone. After you allow it, press and hold again. If you said no, turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › VoiceTag Studio.

I can't hear anything

Turn up the volume with the side buttons. Also check that your iPhone isn't connected to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker in another room.

Are my memories backed up?

They're stored in the app on your iPhone and are included in your iPhone backup. They don't sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes them. To keep a copy, share a memory as a video and save it to Photos.

Can I remove the sample?

Yes. Touch and hold it, then choose Delete. It won't come back.

Privacy Policy

VoiceTag Studio doesn't collect any data. The developer can't see your photos, recordings, or videos. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Photos

You pick photos with the iOS photo picker, and the app receives only the ones you choose. Cutouts are made on your iPhone, and the sticker is stored in the app. The original photo isn't kept.

Microphone and recordings

The microphone is used only while you hold the record button. Recordings are stored in the app on your iPhone.

Sharing

Videos are made on your iPhone and leave it only if you send them. Whatever app you share with handles the video under its own policy. If you choose Save Video, iOS asks for permission to add it to your photo library.

Deleting your data

Touch and hold a sticker to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

The app is meant for adults keeping family memories. It doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
